\section*{Research Summary}
I build HPC-native AI systems that connect scientific intent with data, software, and large-scale computing. At Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, I develop machine-learning methods and inference infrastructure for particle physics as a member of the ATLAS Collaboration at the Large Hadron Collider. My research asks how AI and high-performance computing can reduce the effort between a scientific idea and a reproducible result.
