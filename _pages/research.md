Scientists still spend significant effort translating scientific intent into infrastructure actions. My research brings together AI agents, machine-learning models, and HPC systems to shorten that path while keeping scientific results inspectable and reproducible.

## Agentic AI for scientific workflows

How can scientists delegate computational work while retaining control over scientific decisions? I am developing **HepGateway**, an interaction layer where scientists express an analysis as an executable graph. AI agents act on data, software, and computing resources; explicit conditions govern execution and revision. The research goal is to measure changes in manual intervention, time to result, and provenance coverage.

## Foundation models for scientific data

How much of a simulation's useful structure can a learned representation preserve and transfer? My work on **Foundational Universe** studies tokenization and generative modeling of large three-dimensional scientific fields, using Nyx cosmological simulations. Related work explores token-based models for detector data, track finding, and particle simulation.

[Explore scientific field models]({{ '/projects/#foundational-universe' | relative_url }})

## Scalable inference and HPC integration

How can many physics processes share accelerators effectively? I develop **Inference as a Service** infrastructure and its integration with scientific applications, including ATLAS Athena. This connects model execution to the practical questions of throughput, latency, concurrency, resource utilization, and deployment at HPC facilities.

[Explore inference infrastructure]({{ '/projects/#inference-as-a-service' | relative_url }})

## Physics applications

Particle physics and cosmology supply both the motivation and the tests for this work. My background includes Higgs boson studies with ATLAS, graph-based particle tracking, and generative simulation. Across these applications, computational performance must be evaluated alongside the quality of the scientific result.
